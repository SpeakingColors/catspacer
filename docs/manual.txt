# Intro
Cat Spacer is a spectral sidechain ducker. Instead of ducking the whole sound, it listens to the sidechain (for example a vocal or kick) and cuts only the frequencies in your main track where the sidechain is loud, so both can sit in the same mix. No threshold is needed: it follows the loudest sidechain bands.

# Quick start
- Put Cat Spacer on the sound you want to carve (a lead or pad) and route the sound that should stay clear (vocal, kick) to its sidechain input in your DAW.
- Raise Amount until the gaps appear in the display; the cut curve shows what is being removed.
- Use Low cut and High cut to limit the range that is affected, and Max cut to set the deepest cut.
- Use Audition to hear only what is being removed, Freeze to hold the current sidechain shape.

# Recommendations
- Lead under a vocal: Amount 50 to 70, Attack 5 to 20 ms, Release 100 to 200 ms, Low cut 150 Hz, High cut 8 kHz.
- Pad under a kick: Release shorter (60 to 120 ms), Max cut 8 to 12 dB.
- Keep Floor above 0 if the carving sounds hollow; lower Mix to blend with the untouched sound.
- Latency: Low 512, Normal 1024, High 2048 samples. Use Low while playing live, High for the cleanest cut when mixing.
